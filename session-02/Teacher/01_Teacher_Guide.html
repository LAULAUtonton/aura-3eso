Create Your Character

45–50 minA2+ / B1 startCORE 8REVIEW

Objectives

  • Understand the main information in a short character profile.
  • Review be / have got / can in affirmative and negative sentences.
  • Use 2–3 personality adjectives accurately.
  • Create a short, coherent character seed.

Language

CORE friendly · kind · shy · confident · creative · brave · funny · calm

REVIEW He/She is… · He/She has got… · He/She can/can't…

Appearance language is supported, not expanded as a new lexical list.

Nova · model text

Nova is 14 and she is a creative, confident player. She has got short dark hair and bright blue headphones. She is usually calm, but she can be very funny with her friends. Nova can draw digital characters and she can make short videos. She can’t skateboard very well, but she wants to learn. Her favourite free-time activity is listening to music.

The text deliberately stays within familiar sentence patterns. Students are not expected to reproduce every detail.

Concept check

  1. Is Nova shy? No.
  2. Has she got long hair? No.
  3. Can she draw? Yes.
  4. Can she skateboard well? No.
  5. Which words describe personality? creative, confident, calm, funny

Support & challenge

Support: allow students to select from the word bank and complete sentence starters.

Core: 5–6 accurate sentences using all three target structures.

Challenge: add but or also, plus one reason: “He is brave because…”

Success criteria

  • I use is/isn't for personality or description.
  • I use has got/hasn't got for appearance or possessions.
  • I use can/can't for abilities.
  • I include at least 2 personality adjectives.
  • My partner can understand my character.
