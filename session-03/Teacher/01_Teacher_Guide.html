AURA Session 3 · Teacher Guide

Meet My Character · 3º ESO · Synchronize-style review level
COREREVIEWCP1

Mission: Turn your Character Seed into a clear, natural mini-presentation.

Language: be · have got · can/can't · likes / doesn't like · and / but / also / because.

Target outcome: a rehearsed audio of about 30–45 seconds. Preparation can be richer (7–8 sentences), but the final performance should remain concise and intelligible.

1. Learning aims

Students will...

  • retrieve character language from Session 2;
  • select relevant information rather than list everything;
  • connect ideas with and, but, also, because;
  • improve a first draft after peer feedback;
  • record a short, understandable oral text.

Teacher watches for...

  • subject + verb agreement;
  • correct use of has got;
  • can + base verb;
  • clear pronunciation and pacing;
  • evidence of revision, not memorisation only.

3. Model text

Meet Zia. She is confident and creative, but she is sometimes a little shy. She has got short dark hair and green glasses. She can draw really well and she can also play the guitar. She likes skateboarding and watching series because they help her relax. She doesn't like getting up early. In her team, she is the person who always has a new idea.
Teaching point: This model is deliberately richer than the minimum final checkpoint. Students should notice how ideas are selected and connected, not copy every feature.

4. Language to notice

FunctionUseful formCommon issue
Identity/personalityHe/She is... / He/She is ... but ...Omitting is.
AppearanceHe/She has got...Using have got with he/she.
AbilityHe/She can + verbAdding to after can.
InterestsHe/She likes + -ing / doesn't like + -ingMixing forms.
Connectingand · but · also · becauseWriting isolated sentences only.

5. CP1 assessment

Minimum evidence: 2 personality traits + 1 hobby/sport + 1 ability + clear connected speech. The student's planning may include more information, but the audio should stay focused.
