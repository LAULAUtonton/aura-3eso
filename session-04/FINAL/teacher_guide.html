SESSION 4 OF 11 · CHECKPOINT 1 · 45–50 MIN

AURA Contest · Give Your Aura!

Goal: present a character clearly, understand classmates’ presentations and justify a vote. Evidence: short oral presentation, listening card and reasoned voting.

Language: He/She is… · has got… · can/can’t… · likes… · I vote for… because… · In my opinion… · What about…?
TimeStageTeacher / student action
0–5Mission briefingShow the three award categories. Model a kind, specific reason for a vote.
5–10RehearsalPairs rehearse their 30–45 second character presentation and one follow-up question.
10–30AURA ContestStudents present in groups or stations. Audience completes the listening card.
30–38Ask & reactAudience asks one simple question after each presentation; speaker answers from notes.
38–45VoteEach student gives 100 Aura Points in each category: Best Character Concept, Best English and Best Presentation. Use the Google Form/Sheet if available.
45–50ReflectionStudents finish: “One idea I liked was…” and “Next time I will…”

Assessment checklist

☐ Message is understandable. ☐ Uses at least four relevant sentences. ☐ Includes personality/ability/interests. ☐ Listens and records information. ☐ Gives a respectful reason for a vote.

Support and extension

Support: permit reading from the Character Card, sentence frames and a partner rehearsal. Extension: add a reason with because and respond to an unscripted question. Do not penalise accent.
