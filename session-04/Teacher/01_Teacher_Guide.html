Session 4 of 11 · Choose Your Playstyle

Objective: talk about sports and free-time activities, express preferences and collect real information from a partner.

Language: love / like / enjoy / don't like / hate + -ing; Do you like...?; What do you like doing?; functional play / go / do.

Outcome: pair survey + data collection + short report.

2. Model interaction

A: What do you like doing in your free time?

B: I really enjoy playing video games, and I also like listening to music.

A: Do you like playing any sports?

B: Yes. I like playing basketball, but I don't like running.

A: Why do you like basketball?

B: Because I play with my friends.
