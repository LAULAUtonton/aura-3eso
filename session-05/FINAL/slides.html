FEED vs REALITY

What does Ariann usually do? What is she doing now?

No fixed answer is required on this opening slide. Use it to introduce the mission.

ROUTINE

She posts every Friday.
She practises after school.

always · usually · often · every week

These are routines: Present Simple. The action happens regularly, not only at this moment.
NEXT →

RIGHT NOW

She is recording a song now.
Her friend is filming today.

now · at the moment · today

These actions are happening now or around today: Present Continuous = am/is/are + verb-ing.
