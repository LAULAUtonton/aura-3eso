SESSION 5 OF 11 · 45–50 MIN

Feed vs Reality · Who is Ariann Music?

Goal: distinguish routines from actions happening now in a realistic content-creator context. Evidence: reporter notes and an 80–100-word Feed vs Reality paragraph.

Language: Present Simple vs Present Continuous · always/usually/every day · now/at the moment/today · post, create, edit, record, reply, practise.
TimeStageTeacher / student action
0–5HookShow two statements: “Ariann posts music videos every week” / “She is editing a video now.” Students choose routine or now.
5–12Media reportRead/project the Ariann feed card. Students find who, what she usually does and what she is doing today.
12–20NoticeColour-code Present Simple and Present Continuous. Contrast time markers and verb forms.
20–30Reality CheckTeams sort 10 statements into FEED (routine) / RIGHT NOW (current action), then correct two glitches.
30–42Reporter writingStudents write an 80–100 word paragraph: 3 routine facts, 3 current actions and one contrast with but.
42–50Share / exitPartners underline one correct example of each tense and say one improvement.

Support and extension

Support: sentence bank, colour coding and a 60-word minimum with selected facts. Extension: add a question and an original contrast. Treat errors as information for session 6; do not formally score every grammatical slip.
