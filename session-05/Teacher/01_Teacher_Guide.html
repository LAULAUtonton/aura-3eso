COREREVIEWPAIR SURVEY

Session 5 of 11 · My Daily Grind

Objective: describe everyday routines and ask a partner about frequency using the Present Simple.

Language: Present Simple affirmative/negative/questions + frequency adverbs and expressions.

Vocabulary load: only 6 core routine verbs; free-time vocabulary is recycled from Session 4.

Outcome: personal routine + partner survey + short report.

1. Lesson flow · 45–50 min

TimeStageTeacher actionStudent evidence

3. Grammar board

FunctionModelTeaching note
AffirmativeI get up at seven. / She gets up at seven.Briefly flag 3rd person -s; do not overteach.
NegativeI don't play games before school. / He doesn't play games before school.Base verb after don't/doesn't.
QuestionDo you have breakfast at home?Short answer + extra information.
FrequencyI usually do homework after school.Adverb before main verb.

4. Frequency line

always → usually → often → sometimes → hardly ever → never

Useful time expressions: every day · on weekdays · at weekends · after school · in the evening.

5. Model interaction

A: What time do you get up on weekdays?

B: I usually get up at seven.

A: Do you have breakfast at home?

B: Yes, I do. I always have breakfast at home.

A: How often do you play video games after school?

B: I sometimes play after homework.
